SMART CITY PLATFORM

City Data Platform CDP

The City Data Platform (CDP) is a centralized hub designed to collect, manage and analyze diverse urban data. Its objective is to support efficient city administration, strategic urban development and better quality of life—helping cities move toward a Smart City model.

CDP MODULES & CAPABILITIES

One platform. A connected view of the city.

01

CCTV Map & Real-time View

A central interface for tracking and managing CCTV cameras across the city, keeping location and camera status data current.

  • Map-based camera location and operating status, including online/offline indicators.
  • Search and filter cameras by name, ID, area or operational status.
  • Display real-time camera feeds in compact monitoring windows.
02

Online Identity Verification

A secure online identity verification process that allows people to prove their identity through digital channels without appearing in person before an officer.

03

Monitoring & Alerting

Continuously monitors important city status and operational data in real time or near real time.

  • Operational dashboard
  • Interactive incident map
  • Traffic charts and analytical visualizations
  • Focused point monitoring
04

Geographic Information System (GIS)

GIS at a scale of at least 1:4,000 for urban areas, providing a geographic data foundation for connected city assets and services.

  • Stores spatial locations such as Smart Poles, CCTV, water/weather sensors, waste points and EV charging stations.
  • Displays CDP information on GIS maps for a clear citywide view.
05

Citizen Complaint Management

Enables citizens to communicate problems and needs to responsible agencies more easily, supporting faster and more transparent public-sector response.

06

Smart Pole & Sensor Integration

CDP supports integration with Smart Poles, water-level sensors, weather sensors and other connected urban infrastructure.
